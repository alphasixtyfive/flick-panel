# Flick Panel 0.1.92

- Choose an accent for a tile or graph. You can also name and colour room tiles individually.
- Camera controls adapt to narrow screens, with more space around Close. Use **Test connection** to check a feed and Talk before saving its settings.
- Music tiles show when playback is paused or buffering. The player keeps artwork and volume controls in place.
- The photo clock can show a Home Assistant message and defaults to a five-minute idle timeout.
- Review a backup before restoring it, or save and export your current layout from setup.
- Open setup directly from an empty Favourites page. About links to the developer, manual and source.

Requires Android 9 or later. Choose the universal APK if you are unsure which version to install.
